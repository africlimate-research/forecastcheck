# Code of conduct

ForecastCheck is built by and for forecasters, researchers and engineers across many
countries and institutions. We want everyone who takes part — in issues, pull requests,
reviews or training sessions — to be able to do so without harassment or disrespect.

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. In short:

- Be respectful and welcoming, whatever someone's background, experience or role.
- Give and receive feedback on the work, not the person.
- No harassment, insults, or unwelcome personal attention, in public or in private.
- Respect that people write in a second or third language; ask before assuming.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behaviour that breaks this code, write to
[research@africlimate.ai](mailto:research@africlimate.ai). Reports are read by the
maintainers, kept confidential, and answered. Maintainers who do not follow the code may
be removed from the project.
